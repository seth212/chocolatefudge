Chocolate Fudge

Description

Chocolate fudge is a timeless treat known for its rich flavor and smooth, melt-in-your-mouth texture. Made from a blend of high-quality chocolate, butter, sugar, and cream, this classic confection delivers the perfect balance of sweetness and deep cocoa flavor. Each bite is dense, creamy, and indulgent, making it a favorite dessert for chocolate lovers of all ages.

Our chocolate fudge is crafted with care to ensure every batch has the perfect consistency—soft enough to melt on your tongue, yet firm enough to hold its shape. The slow cooking and careful cooling process create the signature velvety texture that makes traditional fudge so irresistible. Whether enjoyed as an everyday treat or a special dessert, chocolate fudge offers a comforting, nostalgic taste that never goes out of style.

Perfect for gifting, sharing, or savoring on your own, chocolate fudge is a versatile dessert that fits any occasion. Serve it at parties, pair it with coffee or tea, or keep a box on hand whenever you crave something sweet and satisfying. With its rich chocolate flavor and classic homemade feel, chocolate fudge is a dessert that always brings a moment of pure indulgence.

Ingredients

  • High-quality cocoa or melted chocolate
  • Granulated sugar
  • Sweetened condensed milk or heavy cream
  • Unsalted butter
  • Vanilla extract
  • A pinch of salt

Steps

  1. Line a pan with parchment paper.
  2. Heat sugar, butter, and condensed milk in a saucepan, stirring until smooth.
  3. Add chocolate and mix until melted and thick.
  4. Remove from heat and stir in vanilla and salt.
  5. Pour mixture into the pan and spread evenly.
  6. Let cool in the refrigerator until firm.
  7. Cut into squares and serve.
